# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in WebSquared, through issues, pull requests,
discussions, or any other project space, to be a welcoming, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in how you communicate, especially when
  disagreeing about technical decisions.
- Give constructive feedback, and accept it gracefully in return.
- Focus criticism on code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, most misunderstandings come from missing context,
  not bad intent.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in
  a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces, the GitHub repository (issues, pull
requests, discussions), and any other official communication channels
associated with the project, and also applies when someone is
representing the project in public spaces elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a GitHub
issue addressed to the maintainers, or, for sensitive reports, through
GitHub's private reporting tools on the repository. Reports will be
reviewed and addressed as appropriate, which may include a warning,
temporary restriction, or permanent ban from project spaces, at the
maintainers' discretion.

Maintainers are expected to hold themselves to the same standard, and to
apply this Code of Conduct fairly and consistently.
